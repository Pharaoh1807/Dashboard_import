---
description: Automatically update PROJECT_STATUS.md after modifications
---

Whenever a significant change, refactoring, or feature implementation is completed:
1. Review the changes made to the codebase.
2. Open `PROJECT_STATUS.md`.
3. Move completed items from "Roadmap" to "Completed".
4. Add any new enhancements or bug fixes to the "📅 Gần đây" (Recently) subsection.
5. Ensure the "Current Focus" is up to date.
6. This should be done as the final step of the task before reporting completion to the user.
